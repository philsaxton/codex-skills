# Evaluation disposition

This evaluation is retired as a dependency-detection regression. Its frozen oracle treated `S14.1 -> S1.2` as required, but independent adjudication found that prior completion was not necessary for the documentation-only S14.1 work. The historical and counterfactual contracts therefore cannot support the oracle's expected finding.

The investigation instead exposed a portable reviewer failure: references, preservation obligations, historical sequence, and completed authorities can be mistaken for executable prerequisites. The accepted reviewer behavior is to evaluate decision-bearing assertions independently, report material ambiguity without inventing authority, and still identify a missing dependency when evidence establishes that prior completion is necessary.

The frozen artifacts, oracle, and scoring remain unchanged as provenance. Do not use them as an active pass/fail benchmark. The corrected portable skill was behaviorally checked against the historical contract with contemporaneous governing sources, the clear counterfactual, and an unambiguous missing-prerequisite control.
