If a delivery fix is warranted, retain the original failure, identify changed guidance and run a fresh retest; never silently relabel a failed trial. Missing evidence/coverage remains incomplete (P01 tests access, B06 tests missing guidance; additional missing-receipt control may be added as a clearly labelled extension).

## Scoring and integrity

An assessor who did not author candidate or fixture judges raw events, outputs, edits, checks, and identity manifests against these gates. Record disagreements. Separate guidance delivery, skill fit, completion truth, scope/independence, and execution cost. Count full-suite commands by owner/state/reason, separating meaningful red/fix cycles and explicit stage gates from handoff-only duplicates. Direct runs also count; the receipt wrapper is not the only observation source.

A passing deciding workflow requires an actual correct edit, meaningful tests, all required checks satisfied, independent review, preserved boundaries and zero handoff-only full-suite reruns. Boundary cases require truthful limits and appropriate affected checks, not necessarily a passing product. Negative delivery controls remain failed delivery. Global activation requires supported delivery in every intended consuming runtime; CLI results cannot silently satisfy a desktop-only gate.

Retain raw records privately under workspace `artifacts/`; publish reproducible inputs, prompts, guidance identities and a concise result ledger here. Record active installation state before/after, preserve all vendor bytes and source projects. No City of Vice development, installation, publication, or deployment is part of a trial.
